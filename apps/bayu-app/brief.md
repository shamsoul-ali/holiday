# Bayu — Sabah Smart Tourism AI Ecosystem

> Not just a "tourism app" — an AI-powered tourism digital transformation for Sabah.

If you pitch it as just hotspot + packages, it's average. If you pitch it as **AI-powered tourism digital transformation for Sabah**, it becomes BIG.

---

## Current Gap in Sabah Tourism

**Sabah has:**
- World-class nature (islands, mountains, wildlife)
- Strong Chinese, Korean, European tourist market
- Amazing eco-tourism assets

**But lacks:**
- One centralized official tourism super-app
- AI-powered itinerary planner
- Real-time crowd / weather / tide integration
- Unified booking ecosystem
- Data analytics dashboard for tourism board
- Digital monetization funnel

---

## Positioning: "Visit Sabah AI"

Not just an app — **Sabah's Official AI Travel Companion**.

## Core Features (Base Version)

### 1. Attractions Discovery Engine

**Include:**
- Islands (Sipadan, Mabul, Semporna)
- Mountains (Kinabalu)
- Wildlife (Kinabatangan River)
- Cultural villages
- Hidden gems

**Each attraction includes:**
- Best time to visit
- Estimated cost
- Weather integration
- Travel time
- Suggested duration
- Nearby food & hotels

### 2. AI Concierge (Main Differentiator)

**Example use case:**

Tourist types: *"I'm coming 4 days from KL with my wife. Budget RM5,000. We love nature and seafood."*

**AI generates:**
- Day 1: Kota Kinabalu sunset + seafood
- Day 2: Island hopping
- Day 3: Kundasang + Kinabalu Park
- Day 4: Cultural village

**Plus:**
- Budget breakdown
- Estimated transport cost
- Local SIM suggestion
- Weather alerts

This makes Sabah look advanced and smart.

### 3. Integrated Travel Marketplace

**Inside the app:**
- Verified travel agents
- Licensed guides
- Island hopping packages
- Diving packages
- Mount Kinabalu permit bookings

**Sabah Tourism earns:**
- Commission % from bookings
- Listing fees
- Featured placement fees

### 4. Food Intelligence Map

Instead of generic Google ratings, add:
- "Tourist-friendly"
- "Muslim-friendly"
- "Viral spot"
- "Local hidden gem"
- Peak hours indicator
